How it is made

Slow hands, fresh butter

No premixed frosting, no cake sitting in a freezer for a week. Batter goes in the morning of, buttercream is whipped the same day, and decoration happens once the crumb has fully cooled.

Baked to order

Every tier is baked for your date, never pulled from stock.

Finished by hand

Piping, pearls, gold leaf and lettering, all applied in-house.

Delivered standing

We set the cake up at your venue so it arrives the way you saw it.

How to order

Four steps, no stress

Most orders are settled over WhatsApp in under ten minutes. Give us at least 48 hours; for weddings and December dates, book two to three weeks ahead.

Send the brief

Message us the occasion, date, headcount, and any theme or colours you have in mind. A reference photo helps.

Get a quote

We confirm size, flavour and finish, then send a firm price. Nothing changes after this unless you change it.

Pay a deposit

Seventy percent locks your date on the calendar. The balance is due before delivery.

We deliver

Delivered and set up across Lagos Island and Mainland at your stated time, not an hour after it.
